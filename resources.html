Reader Resources

A place to start, from Losing Someone Who Is Still Here

These are resources I've found helpful and mention in the book.

Understanding the diagnosis

  • Lewy Body Dementia Association (LBDA) The primary national organization for LBD education, research, and caregiver support. Includes treatment guidance, medication information, and a caregiver resource center. Lewy Line: 800-539-9767
  • Lewy Body Dementia Resource Center A patient- and caregiver-focused resource with information on symptoms, treatment, stages, and medication safety. Helpline open 12 hours a day, 7 days a week: 833-523-5463
  • National Institute on Aging A government resource with authoritative information on LBD diagnosis, treatment, and management.

Finding support

  • LBDA support group directory A national directory of in-person and virtual support groups for caregivers and families.
  • LBD Resource Center support groups Virtual support groups on Zoom several times a month, including groups for caregivers and for people living with LBD.
  • Psychology Today therapist directory Filter by the Alzheimer's/dementia specialty to find a therapist trained in dementia care.
  • Mental Health and Aging Connects families with geriatric mental health providers.

Legal and financial planning

  • National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys Find an elder law attorney with Medicaid planning expertise.
  • Medicaid long-term care planning Plain-language information on Medicaid eligibility, the 5-year look-back period, and long-term care planning.
